# AutoApplyMAX Privacy Policy

**Draft for Chrome Web Store publication**

**Effective date:** June 8, 2026

AutoApplyMAX is a browser extension that helps users fill job application forms. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it is stored, and what limited information may be sent to the AutoApplyMAX community mapping service.

## Data Stored On Your Device

AutoApplyMAX stores the following data locally in Chrome extension storage:

- Profile and job-application answers entered by the user
- Resume metadata and content added to the profile; content is kept in extension-owned IndexedDB
- Extension settings
- Learned field mappings
- Application history, including job title, company, URL, ATS, and timestamp

This local data is used to fill forms and provide extension features. It is not uploaded to the AutoApplyMAX community service. Data entered into a job application is provided directly to the job site when the user reviews and submits that site's form, subject to the job site's own privacy policy.

Users can edit or clear locally stored mappings and application history from the extension. Removing the extension also removes its Chrome-managed local storage, subject to browser behavior.

## Community Mapping Service

AutoApplyMAX may send and retrieve reusable field-mapping metadata through a centrally operated community service. A mapping can include:

- The application site or ATS identifier
- A normalized field signature that excludes the raw CSS selector
- The generic profile-field key associated with that field

Mapping submissions must not contain profile values, resume contents, completed application answers, or application history. Submitted mappings are manually moderated before they are made available to other users. Submission does not guarantee publication.

The service may process ordinary technical request data, such as IP address, request time, and error or security logs, as necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. This technical data is not used to build advertising profiles.

## Permissions And Website Access

AutoApplyMAX requests Chrome permissions needed to:

- Store extension data locally
- access the active tab and supported application pages
- inject the autofill scripts requested by the user
- display the extension's side panel
- download the stored resume only after a user clicks the resume helper

Website access is used to identify and fill job application fields and to learn reusable field mappings. AutoApplyMAX does not use website access to collect general browsing history.

## Data Sharing And Sale

AutoApplyMAX does not sell personal data. It does not share local profile data, resume contents, application answers, or application history with advertisers or data brokers.

Moderated field-mapping metadata may be distributed to other AutoApplyMAX users because that is the purpose of the community mapping service. Service providers may process limited data on behalf of AutoApplyMAX only as needed to host, secure, or maintain the service.

## Retention And Security

Local data remains in Chrome extension storage until the user clears it or removes the extension, subject to browser behavior.

Community mapping submissions may be retained while they remain useful for moderation, abuse prevention, and delivery of the mapping service. Technical logs may be retained for a limited period needed for security and operations.

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect the community service, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

AutoApplyMAX is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when AutoApplyMAX features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected by updating this document and its effective date.
